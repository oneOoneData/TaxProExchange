# TaxDome MCP: Your AI Assistant Just Got Eyes on Your Practice

Your practice management software has a problem: it knows everything about your firm, but it only answers the questions you know to ask.

You want to know what's overdue this week, who hasn't paid, or where the Smith return is stuck — but the answer is buried behind five clicks in a dashboard you have to remember to open. The information is there. It's just not *reachable*.

TaxDome is about to change that. The company behind one of the most widely used practice management platforms in tax is building an **MCP server** — and it's the closest thing yet to giving your AI assistant a real job at your firm.

## What MCP Actually Is (Skip This if You Already Know)

MCP stands for **Model Context Protocol** — an open standard that lets AI assistants plug into other software, the same way a USB-C port lets one cable charge a laptop, a phone, and a headset. Anthropic introduced it in late 2024; OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft have all adopted it since.

Before MCP, your ChatGPT or Claude could write a great email about a client's filing status — but it had no idea what that status actually was. It was smart and blind. MCP is what gives it eyes.

Here's the one-sentence version: **MCP turns an AI chatbot into an AI teammate by letting it read and act on your real systems, under your permissions.**

## What TaxDome's MCP Server Does

TaxDome announced its MCP server this year and opened a waitlist for early access. From the company's own description, the capabilities are:

- **Connect Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI tool to TaxDome in minutes** — no custom integrations, no developer required
- **Query client data, deadlines, and job status in natural language** — "What's overdue this week?" gets a real answer, not a suggestion to check the dashboard
- **Go further than answers** — have the AI take action, like updating records and moving work forward
- **Every connection runs on your TaxDome permissions** — the AI can only see and do what your user account can
- **TaxDome stays the system of record** — the AI is a front door, not a replacement database

The waitlist page is live, and it's available first to Professional and Business plan subscribers. In other words: this isn't a far-off roadmap item. It's shipping.

## What This Unlocks for a Small Firm

### 1. The morning triage becomes one sentence

Right now, "what's happening in my practice today?" means opening pipelines, checking task lists, scanning the inbox, and mentally reconciling three different views. With MCP, it's a single chat message. The AI reads your jobs and tasks and comes back with: *three returns are overdue, the Patel engagement hasn't moved in nine days, and two invoices are past due — want me to draft follow-ups?*

That's not a dashboard improvement. That's a different way of running the firm.

### 2. Questions you never asked because they were too expensive to answer

Some questions are technically answerable in TaxDome but practically not worth the clicking: *How many of my clients are pass-through entities? Which engagements ran over budget last year? Who's still missing their organizer?* When the cost of an answer drops to a chat message, you start asking questions that actually improve how the firm runs.

### 3. Action without app-switching

The step beyond answering is doing. MCP lets the AI update records, create tasks, and move work forward — inside the tools you already use. Your staff stop being data-entry operators between systems and start being reviewers of work the AI stages for them.

### 4. The dashboard stops being the interface

This is the quiet revolution. Every practice management vendor is racing to become "AI-native" — and MCP is the standard that makes it possible. TaxDome isn't the only one, but it's one of the first major tax-specific platforms to ship it. When your AI assistant becomes the front door to your practice data, the question of which assistant you use (Claude, ChatGPT, something else) matters more than which dashboard skin you pick.

## The Question Every Firm Should Ask First

Client data plus AI equals one conversation you need to have before the first query: **security.**

TaxDome's stated approach is the right one — connections run on your existing permissions, and TaxDome remains the system of record. But "the AI can do what you can do" cuts both ways. Before you connect, take five minutes to:

- **Review what permissions your staff accounts actually hold.** The AI inherits them. If your admin account has god-mode access, so does the AI using it.
- **Start read-only.** Query first. Give the AI write access to a sandbox or a small set of workflows before turning it loose on live client records.
- **Remember Circular 230 still applies.** Confidentiality and professional standards don't pause because a chatbot is doing the retrieving. Make sure whatever AI you connect is one whose data handling you're comfortable with.

## What to Do Next

1. **Join the waitlist.** It's free, it's live, and early access usually means a chance to shape the product. (Search "TaxDome MCP waitlist" — it's on their site.)
2. **Audit your permissions now**, before the access arrives, so you're not scrambling later.
3. **Pick your first three workflows.** What would you actually ask your practice software every morning? Those are the questions you'll automate first.

## The Takeaway

MCP is the missing link between "AI that gives good advice" and "AI that knows your practice." TaxDome shipping an MCP server is a signal that the era of the blind chatbot is ending — and that the firms who learn to work with an AI teammate that can *see* their data are the ones who'll run leaner, faster, and with fewer all-hands-on-deck fire drills during busy season.

The technology is ready. The question is whether you've decided what you want your AI assistant to do when it gets eyes on your practice.
